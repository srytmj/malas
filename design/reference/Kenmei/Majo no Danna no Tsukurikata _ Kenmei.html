Synopsis

Witches seek the "strongest husband" in order to produce a worthy successor capable of maintaining the seal of the Dark Lord.

Radi, a witch unable to use magic, encounters a young gladiator named Otto and becomes captivated by his swordsmanship.

A battle fantasy about a low-ranking witch and a rookie gladiator as they strive to raise the ultimate husband into the strongest of all!
